# Awesome Claude 5.5 Videos

Files here are copied from https://github.com/athemeroy/awesome-claude-5-5-videos (original writing and tables: CC BY 4.0, by athemeroy; see `THIRD_PARTY.md` for what the licence does not cover). This wrapper was written in this repository; it is not part of upstream. The collection's own count is as of 2026-10-11.

- `README.md`: overview, findings and the "Make-your-own" section.
- `docs/library.md`: the full directory by model. `docs/domain-style-atlas.md`: by content and style. `docs/README.md` lists every document.
- `data/*.csv`, `data/*.json`: the case tables behind the documents.

Not copied: `assets/` (stills from creators' posts, which upstream excludes from its licence) and `scripts/`. Image links in the copied Markdown therefore do not resolve here; the linked original posts do. Model attribution in this collection is partly creator-reported, as the documents state; repeat that caveat when citing a case.
